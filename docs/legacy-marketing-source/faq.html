About HomeTutor

What is HomeTutor?

HomeTutor is an online learning platform based on 21st century education. The concept of learning is revision exercises and drill questions through effective multimedia learning and capable of stimulating children to think creatively.

Children are exposed with syllabus according to the latest curriculum standards the same as what is learned in schools starting from primary school to secondary school such as Lower Primary, UPSR, PT3 and SPM.

Why is HomeTutor different from other online learning?

HomeTutor e-Learning unlike the others because the questions provided in HomeTutor exceeding 70,000++ that 100% follow latest curriculum standard suggested by the Ministry of Education.

HomeTutor also has collaborations with universities in Malaysia such as Sultan Idris Education University (UPSI) and National University of Malaysia (UKM).

In addition, HomeTutor also regularly has discussion with the headmasters in district of Hulu Langat, Selangor to improve the systems’ function and consistently follow latest curriculum standard.

How can I get the access after my subscription?

All questions are given through portal. Student just need to login into the portal to make the exercises.

HomeTutor can be accessed using various devices such as:
a) Laptops
b) Smartphone
c) Notebooks
d) MacBook
e) iPad
f) Tablets
g) Personal Computer
